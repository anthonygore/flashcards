# Requirments

## Problem

The outcome and why it matters

“What prompted this request? How is it handled today? What would success look like?”

## People

Users, collaborators and decision-makers

“Who uses this? Can someone walk me through a recent example? Who owns the process and validates the result?”

## Rules

Data, permissions and exceptions

“Which system is authoritative? Who can see or change what? What happens when information is missing or an operation fails?”

## Plan

The smallest useful solution and delivery approach

“What’s essential first? What already exists? What assumptions should we test before building?”

## Proof

How you establish correctness and introduce it safely

“What examples demonstrate success? What must never happen? Who will trial it, and how will we monitor it?”
